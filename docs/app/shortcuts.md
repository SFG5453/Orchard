# Keyboard shortcuts

Settings, Shortcuts shows the same list in the app. Orchard turns playback shortcuts off while Spotlight search or Settings is open, and while you type in a text box.

## Shortcut list

- `Space`: play or pause. Media Play does the same.
- `Left` and `Right`: jump 5 seconds back or forward in the current song.
- Media Prev and Media Next: previous or next song in the queue.
- `F`: open or close the fullscreen player. It needs a loaded song and does nothing while the music video player is open.
- `Esc`: close the music video player, the fullscreen player, Spotlight, Quick Search, a popup, or Settings, or go back to the previous page.
- `/` or Ctrl+K: open Spotlight search.
- Any letter or number on a loaded playlist page: open Quick Search for that playlist.
- `Up` and `Down` inside Spotlight or Quick Search: move through the results.
- `Enter` inside Spotlight or Quick Search: open the highlighted result in Spotlight, or play the highlighted song in Quick Search.
- Double-click the title bar: maximize or restore the window.

## Play or pause with the keyboard

Press Space or the Media Play key. Space does nothing while you type in a text box or while Spotlight or Settings is open.

## Seek with the keyboard

Press the Left arrow key to go back 5 seconds and the Right arrow key to go forward 5 seconds. The arrow keys do nothing while you type in a text box or while Spotlight or Settings is open.

## Skip songs with the keyboard

Press the Media Prev or Media Next key on your keyboard. Orchard also answers to the media controls of your operating system.

## Open the fullscreen player with the keyboard

Press F while a song is loaded. Press F again or press Esc to close it. See [Play music](playback.md).

## Open Spotlight with the keyboard

Press `/` or Ctrl+K. The `/` key does nothing while you type in a text box. Ctrl+K works while you type in a text box. See [Search and Spotlight](search.md).

## Search inside a playlist with the keyboard

Open a playlist page, wait for its songs to load, and type a letter or number. Quick Search opens. Use Up and Down to move through matches, Enter to play, and Esc to close. See [Search and Spotlight](search.md).

## Go back or close a popup

Press Esc. It closes search windows and popups, closes the fullscreen player, and returns from an album, artist, or playlist page to the previous page.

## Maximize or restore the window

Double-click the title bar. Orchard uses its own title bar, and the double-click toggles between maximized and restored.
