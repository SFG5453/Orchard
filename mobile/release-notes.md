## Orchard Mobile 1.3.1 "Legumes Subaltern"

### Fixes
- **Songs Appearing Twice**: The same song kept turning up twice in a row in the queue. Autoplay tops the queue up when three tracks are left, and it was writing back the list of upcoming songs as it had been *before* the recommendations were fetched. If a track finished while that request was in flight, the song that had just started playing was put straight back into the queue behind itself. Every top-up added another pair.
- **Album Queues Emptying**: Playing an album could leave the queue showing almost nothing. When a beat-matched transition ran between two album tracks, playback handed over to a queue containing only the mix and the next song, and everything after it was dropped. The queue was then saved that way, so it was still gone after restarting the app.
- **Next Playing Something Else**: With shuffle on, Next played a song that was not in the queue at all. Orchard shuffles by reordering the queue itself, which is the order the list shows, but the player was *also* running its own separate shuffle, and that was the one deciding what came next. The two no longer disagree.
- **Turning Shuffle Off**: Switching shuffle off left the queue shuffled, because reordering it had thrown the original order away. Orchard now remembers the order shuffle was turned on over and puts it back, including after restarting the app. Anything queued or added by Autoplay while shuffled stays at the end rather than being shuffled into the middle of the restored order. Shuffling an album or playlist from its page is included: switching shuffle off afterwards gives you the original running order.
- **Repeated Album Intros**: A song a beat-matched transition had already mixed into would replay the seconds the mix covered if you went back to it later in the queue.
